Predict first · Softmax

A decode pass gives three logits: 3, 1, -2. You want probabilities. What do you expect if you divide each logit by their total?

Your thinking: “dividing by the sum should make them add to one?” · 70% sure

Dividing works for the sum, but not for the sign: 3/2 = 1.5 and -2/2 = -1 are not probabilities. SoftmaxSoftmaxlearning · 60% Turns a vector of scores into probabilities that sum to 1. In booksUnderstanding Deep Learning, p. 72 · Inference Engineering, p. 226 Studied in2.2 LLM Inference Mechanics (1/2) Leads toLogits and token selection · Scaled dot-product attention Show on the map fixes it in one extra step: make every score positive, then divide by the total.

p_i = \frac{e^{z_i}}{\sum_j e^{z_j}}

For 3, 1, -2: e^3 \approx 20.1, e^1 \approx 2.7, e^{-2} \approx 0.14. The total is about 22.9, so the probabilities are about 0.88, 0.12, 0.006.
