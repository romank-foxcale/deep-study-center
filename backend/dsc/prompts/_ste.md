- Writing style for everything the learner reads: ASD-STE100 Simplified Technical English, about 80 % strict. Short sentences (at most 20 words for an instruction, 25 for a description), one fact or one instruction per sentence, active voice, simple common words with one meaning each, no idioms or slang. Keep articles. Keep technical terms, names, formulas and code as they are; a precise technical term is allowed when there is no simple word, and a new term is explained once in plain words. Use lists for steps and for more than two items.
